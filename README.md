## Before submitting your product

1. Read the platform's current submission guidelines.
2. Confirm that your product fits the category.
3. Check whether submission is free, paid, or invitation-only.
4. Use an accurate product description, logo, screenshots, and contact details.
5. Do not submit duplicate listings or misleading claims.
6. Track referral visits and sign-ups so you can see which directories are useful.

## SEO note

A listing does not automatically provide a dofollow backlink, improve Domain Rating, or increase Google rankings. Some platforms use `nofollow` or `sponsored` links, and some pages may not be indexed. Prioritize relevant platforms that can bring real visitors and customers over raw backlink counts.
